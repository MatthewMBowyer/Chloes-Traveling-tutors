Every child learns differently

No two children are the same. That is why we adapt our teaching to suit how each learner thinks, understands, remembers, and grows.

By matching our teaching to the way your child learns best, we help them build understanding, confidence, and a genuine love of learning.

◉

Visual learners

Learn best through images, diagrams, colour, examples, and clear visual structure.

♫

Auditory learners

Understand best by listening, discussing, repeating, and explaining aloud.

✎

Read/write learners

Learn best through reading notes, writing ideas down, lists, textbooks, and written explanations.

✋

Kinesthetic learners

Thrive with hands-on activities, movement, practice, real-world examples, and doing.

Tutoring questions, answered

Do you offer online tutoring in South Africa?

Yes. 13 of our 16 tutors teach online, so a learner anywhere in South Africa can be matched with a tutor. Several tutors also teach in person in Gauteng, KwaZulu-Natal and Western Cape.

Which areas do you cover?

In person we cover Gauteng, KwaZulu-Natal and the Western Cape. Our tutors are based in areas including Randburg, Northcliff, Featherbrooke, Ruimsig and Krugersdorp in Gauteng; Durban North, Ballito and Salt Rock in KwaZulu-Natal; and Table View, Blouberg and Durbanville in the Western Cape. The full list is in the Where we tutor list above. Online lessons are available anywhere in South Africa, so travel is never a reason to go without support.

What grades and subjects do you tutor?

We tutor Grades 1 to 12, including matric, and first-year university students. Subjects include mathematics, mathematical literacy, physical sciences, life sciences, English, Afrikaans, accounting, business studies, economics, history, geography, computer applications technology, visual arts and study skills.

How do I find the right tutor for my child?

Complete the enquiry form with your child's grade, the subjects they need and your area, for example Randburg, Featherbrooke, Durban North or Table View. We will be in touch to discuss their needs and match them with the right tutor. You can also complete the optional learning preferences assessment, which helps us understand how your child learns best.

How much does tutoring cost?

Fees depend on the tutor, the subject and the grade, so contact us for current rates. Every new learner gets 20% off their first lesson.

Is the learning preferences assessment required?

No, it is optional. It is 12 short questions written from your child's point of view, and it is not a diagnosis. It simply shows whether your child responds best to visual, auditory, read/write or kinesthetic teaching, so we can match them more accurately.

Is tutoring only for learners who are struggling?

No. Families also come to us for exam preparation, confidence building, homework support and extension work for learners who want to be stretched further.
